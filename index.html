The offer

Every brand sells the same two things, on the same calendar, so pros and scheduling work one way everywhere.

One-time job

Customer sends photos, gets a price, picks a day and time window. Charged after the job.

Repeat plan

Every 4 or 12 weeks, on the customer's trash day. Charged after each visit. No contract. Cancel online in one step. Every plan starts with a one-time job, and the plan is offered by email after the job, never by the pro at the door.

Where the plan is used: bin cleaning (core) and junk for property managers. One-time only: yard cleanups (bought twice a year, with a "remind me next season" option). Waits: lawn mowing (bought every 1 to 2 weeks), snow (runs on storms), detailing plans (the car is not home on trash day), commercial cleaning.

Rules of the house

Your standing rules

  1. Every job meets or beats the margin floor.
  2. Every brand runs the same business model.
  3. Customers never see the pro network name.
  4. $0 spending until a brand earns.
  5. No cold calls or cold emails to customers.
  6. Battle tested before anything goes live.
  7. Big questions get dug into, then attacked, before deciding.
  8. Think big: volume wins. Every project is built to earn $1M+ a year.
  9. Every site, portal, email and text works in Spanish and other languages, for customers and for pros.

Already locked by the team

  • The system blocks any job that would break the margin floor.
  • Every customer is shown the Google review link; bad ratings come to you first.
  • Every booking records where the customer came from.
  • Customers pick a day and time window.
  • Card hold placed close to the job.
  • One background timer runs offers, payouts and reminders.
  • All 115 bugs from testing fixed before launch.
  • No paid ads for the first 60 days.
  • Daily encrypted backups, proven by a full wipe and restore.
